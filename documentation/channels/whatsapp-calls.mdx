---
title: "WhatsApp calls"
description: "Answer calls to your Twilio WhatsApp number with a published voice agent."
---

A customer taps **call** in their WhatsApp chat with your number, and one of
your voice agents answers. The call is an ordinary voice call from then on: it
is recorded, analysed and billed like a phone call, it can end the call and
press keypad buttons, and it appears under **Calls** as **WhatsApp**.

Calls work on numbers connected [through Twilio](/channels/whatsapp). Gupshup
numbers do not offer them yet.

## Before you start

- The number is already a [WhatsApp integration](/channels/whatsapp) on
  **Twilio**.
- Your WhatsApp Business Account has a **messaging limit of at least 2,000**.
  Meta grants it after Business Verification; below it, Twilio refuses to turn
  calling on.
- Calling is not already set up on the sender in Twilio. If its **Voice Endpoint
  Configuration** points at another TwiML app, Talqing will not replace it.
- A **published voice agent** whose **Past conversations** setting is **Continue
  the same conversation on every call** (`conversation.context: "transcript"`), and whose `required`
  [variables](/agents/variables) all have defaults — a call carries no request
  to supply them.

## Turn calls on

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enable the calls trigger">
    In **WhatsApp → Numbers**, pick a published voice agent in the number's
    **Calls** cell — or over the API:

    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://api.in.talqing.com/v1/integrations/$INTEGRATION_ID/triggers \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $TALQING_API_KEY" \
      -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
      -d '{
        "trigger_type": "whatsapp.call.inbound",
        "agent_id": "'"$VOICE_AGENT_ID"'",
        "enabled": true
      }'
    ```

    Talqing creates a TwiML app named `Talqing WhatsApp calls +<number>` in your
    Twilio account and sets it on the sender. Nothing else in Twilio or Meta
    needs touching.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call the number">
    The call button appears in the chat with your number — reopen the chat's
    info page if it has not shown up yet. Tap it; the agent greets you.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Disabling the trigger clears the sender's voice configuration and deletes the
app, and the call button disappears. Disabling or deleting the integration does
the same; enabling the integration again turns calling back on.

## One thread per person

A call and a chat with the same person are **one conversation**. The agent
answering the call sees the chat so far, and the text agent's next reply sees
the call. That is why the voice agent must keep the full transcript: with
`summary` or `none`, a call would start a conversation of its own, and the chat
would carry on in it without its history.

The voice agent sees the same userdata the chat does, including a
[batch](/whatsapp/creating-a-batch) row's columns:

| Variable | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `{{userdata.whatsapp.phone}}` | The caller's number, E.164 |
| `{{userdata.whatsapp.profile_name}}` | Their WhatsApp profile name |
| `{{system_vars.human_phone_number}}` | The caller's number |
| `{{system_vars.agent_phone_number}}` | Your WhatsApp number |

A caller who hides their number on WhatsApp shows as **WhatsApp user** in
**Calls**, and has no phone number variables.

## What a WhatsApp call cannot do

**Transfer to a phone number.** Meta does not allow a WhatsApp call to be
connected to the phone network. A [transfer](/tools/transfer) fails with *calls
on WhatsApp can't be transferred to a phone number*, and its `on_failure` runs
as you set it.

## What it costs

| Who | What |
| --- | --- |
| Twilio, on your Twilio bill | The WhatsApp leg ($0.005/min) and the SIP leg to Talqing ($0.004/min) |
| Meta | Nothing for a call the customer starts |
| Talqing | The voice platform fee, as for any voice call |

Your AI providers bill you for the call as they do for any other.

## When something goes wrong

| What you see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `400` on enable: `WhatsApp calls work only on numbers connected through Twilio` | The integration is on Gupshup. |
| `400` on enable: `Calling on this number is already set up elsewhere in Twilio. Turn it off there first.` | The sender's Voice Endpoint Configuration points at another app. Clear it in Twilio, then enable again. |
| `400` on enable: `could not turn on calling in Twilio: …` | Twilio refused the sender update; its message says why. Below the 2,000 messaging limit is the usual cause. |
| `400` on enable: `an agent answering WhatsApp calls must keep past conversations as the full transcript …` | Set the agent's **Past conversations** to **Continue the same conversation on every call**, and publish. |
| `400` on enable: `'<name>' is required and has no default …` | Give the variable a default, or make it optional. |
| `502` on disable: `Calls are off in Talqing, but Twilio refused to turn them off on the number …` | Usually a rotated Auth Token. Clear the sender's Voice Endpoint Configuration in Twilio. |
| A call listed as *Calls are turned off on this WhatsApp number* | It arrived while the trigger or the integration was off — usually just after turning calls off, before WhatsApp hid the button. |
| A call listed as *WhatsApp did not say who was calling* | Twilio sent no caller. |
| A call listed as *This workspace was out of credits* | Top up; the next call connects. |
| A call listed as *The call rang without ever connecting* | The caller hung up before the call reached the agent. |
