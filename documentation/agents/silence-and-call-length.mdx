---
title: "Silence and call length"
description: "Checking in on a caller who goes quiet, hanging up when they never answer, and the per-agent limit on how long a call may last."
---

Two settings end a call that nobody hung up: `config.silence` for a caller who
goes quiet and stays quiet, and `config.max_duration_seconds` for a call that has
run too long. Without them, an off-hook handset, hold music or an IVR talking to
your agent would keep billing your models until the platform's three-hour cap.

Voice and video only. A [chat](/channels/text-conversations) has no inactivity
timeout and no length limit — it stays open until something ends it — so a text
agent ignores both. They are kept, so switching back to voice restores them.

Both are read from **the agent that answered the call**, like `max_steps`. A
[handoff](/tools/handoff-operation) does not change them.

## Silence

After `silence.timeout` seconds of quiet — counted from when the agent stops
talking — the agent checks whether the caller is still there. It does this **in
its own words**, written by the agent's model, so the check-in comes out in the
language and tone of the conversation rather than as a fixed English line.

It waits `timeout` again after each check-in. Once `max_check_ins` have gone
unanswered, the agent says a short goodbye and hangs up, and the call closes with
`close_reason: silence_timeout`. Anything the caller says resets the count.

`max_check_ins: 0` skips the check-in: the agent says goodbye and hangs up at the
first silence.

Each check-in is recorded as a `caller.check_in` event on the call, with the
`attempt` number and the total (`of`), so a call detail page shows why the agent
spoke up. The check-in itself is in the transcript like any other agent line.

Nothing is checked while a [transfer](/telephony/transfers) holds the call — the
caller is on hold music then, and the quiet is ours, not theirs.

## Call length

`max_duration_seconds` is the longest a call may last, counted from when the
agent starts speaking. At the limit the agent says a brief goodbye, written by its
model, and hangs up. The call closes with `close_reason: max_duration`.

`null` means the platform's own cap of three hours, which applies to every voice
and video call — phone, web and media stream alike. A limit that lands while a
transfer is still dialling waits for the transfer to finish.

## Reference

| Field | Type | Default | Range |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `silence.enabled` | boolean | `true` | |
| `silence.timeout` | number (seconds) | 10 | 5–600 |
| `silence.max_check_ins` | integer | 2 | 0–5 |
| `max_duration_seconds` | integer or null | 1800 | 30–10800; `null` = 10800 |

<Tip>
If callers legitimately go quiet — reading out a long number, or holding for
their bank — raise `silence.timeout` rather than turning silence handling off.
</Tip>
