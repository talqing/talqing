description: "What a WhatsApp batch needs before it can send: a connected number, an approved template, and people who opted in."
---

A WhatsApp batch sends one approved template to every row of a CSV, from a
number you connected under [WhatsApp → Numbers](/channels/whatsapp). When someone
replies, the agent on that number answers — it already knows the row's columns
and the template it "sent".

## Before your first batch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect the number">
    Follow [WhatsApp](/channels/whatsapp) to connect a Twilio or Gupshup sender.
    Enable inbound messages with a published text agent if you want replies
    answered; without one, replies are still counted but nobody answers them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a template approved at your BSP">
    Talqing sends templates; it does not create them. Build one in Twilio's
    Content Template Builder or your Gupshup app and submit it to WhatsApp. Once
    it is approved it appears in the batch form. Twilio templates may carry a
    media header, a footer and buttons; Gupshup templates must be text-only for
    now. See [which templates](/whatsapp/creating-a-batch#which-templates).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rules Meta enforces

**Opt-in is mandatory.** You may only message people who gave you their number
and agreed to hear from you on WhatsApp. Messaging anyone else breaks
[WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy](https://business.whatsapp.com/policy) and
puts your number at risk.

**Categories.** Every template is `marketing`, `utility` or `authentication`,
and Meta keeps re-checking approved ones. A promotional template submitted as
utility is approved as marketing instead, and repeated miscategorization can
cost you the ability to send utility templates. A first message to a prospect is
always marketing.

**US numbers never receive marketing templates.** The row fails with Twilio
error `63049`. The batch form warns when a marketing template meets `+1`
numbers.

**Per-person marketing limit.** Meta caps the marketing templates any one person
receives, based on how they engage. Hitting it also fails the row (`63049` on
Twilio, `131049` from Meta). Don't retry it the same day.

**Messaging limit.** Your business portfolio can start conversations with 250
unique people a day, rising to 2,000, 10,000, 100,000 and unlimited as your
quality holds. Every number in the portfolio shares it — which is why a batch's
daily cap defaults to 250.
